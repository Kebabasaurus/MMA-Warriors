========================================================== MMA GAME
IDEAS Staff, Scouting, Academy & AI Improvements
==========================================================

  ---------------------------
  1. OPTIONAL SCOUTING MODE
  ---------------------------

Scouting should be an optional gameplay feature.

Game Option - OFF - All fighter attributes visible immediately. -
Classic management mode.

-   ON
    -   Fighter information must be discovered.
    -   Unknown stats remain hidden or estimated.
    -   Scouts reveal information over time.

Basic Scout (2 weeks) - Reveals a partial profile. - Amount revealed
depends on scout quality. - Provides enough information for an initial
signing decision.

Full Scout (6 weeks) - Reveals the complete fighter profile. - Removes
all uncertainty. - Unlocks hidden strengths, weaknesses and detailed
notes.

Fight Observation Bonus - If a fighter competes while being scouted: -
Their profile immediately becomes fully revealed. - The live fight gives
enough information to complete the scouting report.

  ---------------------------------
  2. RECRUITMENT & SCOUTING STAFF
  ---------------------------------

Recruitment staff should function similarly to Football Manager.

Staff Attributes - Fighter Judging - Potential Judging - Efficiency -
Regional Knowledge - Networking - Reliability - Negotiation -
Professionalism

Scout Quality - Elite Scout: Reveals 70–90% of useful information after
2 weeks. - Average Scout: Reveals around 40–60%. - Poor Scout: Reveals
only 20–35%.

Hidden Information Better Scouts Reveal Earlier - Fight IQ - Chin -
Cardio - Mental Toughness - Injury Proneness - Work Ethic -
Coachability - Potential - Personality - Consistency

Long-Term Benefits - Discover better prospects. - Find talent earlier. -
Build regional knowledge. - Develop gym contacts. - Produce higher
quality reports. - Reduce expensive signing mistakes.

  ---------------------
  3. FIGHTING ACADEMY
  ---------------------

Purpose Create a long-term youth development pathway similar to Football
Manager’s youth academy.

Purchase & Running Costs - Initial purchase cost. - Weekly operating
costs. - Upgradeable facilities.

Academy Capacity - Limited fighter slots. - Additional slots must be
purchased. - Expanding capacity increases weekly costs.

Academy Scouts - Search for young prospects instead of existing
fighters. - Assign to world regions. - Approximately 2 months before
prospects appear. - Better scouts find better talent.

Generated Prospects - Ages 12–15. - Hidden potential and traits.

Academy Talent List - Sign prospects before rivals do.

Academy Fighters - Train. - Develop. - Fight amateur bouts. - Eventually
earn promotion.

Training Plans - Wrestling - Boxing - Muay Thai - BJJ - Judo - Sambo -
Clinch - Cardio - Strength - Fight IQ

Automatic Development - AI manages development if the player chooses.

Simplified Academy Weight Classes - Broad amateur divisions for easier
matchmaking.

Professional Promotion - Assigned their proper adult weight class. -
Academy history remains intact.

  -------------------------------
  6. PROMOTION AI CARD BUILDING
  -------------------------------

Rebuild AI card construction.

Goals: - Use stars correctly. - Keep the roster active. - Respect
injuries and layoffs. - Avoid repeatedly booking the same fighters. -
Build realistic event hierarchies.

Include activity tracking, inactivity logic, headliner scoring,
matchmaking pools, and AI booking reasons for debugging.

  ------------------------------------
  7. MISSED WEIGHT & CANCELLED BOUTS
  ------------------------------------

Missing weight should trigger multiple possible outcomes instead of
automatically cancelling fights.

Possible outcomes: - Catchweight - Purse fine - Title implications -
Replacement fighter - Postponement - Cancellation

Automatic Rebooking - Search future scheduled events. - Confirm both
fighters are available. - Preserve original card position (Main Event,
Co-Main, Main Card, Prelims, Early Prelims). - If no suitable slot
exists, keep the bout awaiting rebooking.

  ------------------------------
  8. FIGHTER DEVELOPMENT AUDIT
  ------------------------------

Audit the current development system.

Ensure fighters continue improving through: - Training - Coaching - Gym
quality - Age - Potential - Work ethic

Fights should accelerate development rather than being the only source.

Important: If the current system already satisfies these requirements,
do not change it. Only modify areas that fail the audit.
