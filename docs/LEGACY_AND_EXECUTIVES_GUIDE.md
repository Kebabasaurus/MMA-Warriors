# Legacy And Executives Guide

## Fighter Legacy

Fighter legacy is calculated from wins, win percentage, peak overall, popularity, title shots, title wins, title defenses, awards, knockdowns, and recorded rivalry bouts. Retirement evaluates this score for Hall of Fame induction.

Title histories remain attached to each company belt. Winning a vacant or held belt records a title win; retaining the same belt on a later title bout records a defense. Awards and rivalry bouts are career milestones, not temporary news items.

The Results screen includes the Legacy Ledger. It ranks active and retired fighters by all-time legacy and shows company eras alongside them.

## Company Eras

Every AI promotion has a named executive with an archetype, aggression, patience, financial discipline, board security, and tenure. Their company strategy still controls its broad identity, while the executive changes the intensity of its behavior.

Board reviews occur quarterly. Weak stability, financial stress, and failure to meet a company’s apparent level create pressure. A replacement executive records a new era in the company history and writes a Chronicle story.

End-of-year awards update company-era records and company legacy. This means a promotion can build a visible historical identity even when the player is not controlling it.
