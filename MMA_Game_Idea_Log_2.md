# MMA Game — Idea Log #2

## 1. Media and Broadcast System Overhaul

Promotions must actively sign media-rights agreements rather than receiving automatic exposure.

### Media contracts
- Contracts are based on a fixed number of events.
- Without an active agreement, promotions receive lower exposure, revenue, popularity growth, fighter exposure, and sponsorship value.

### Media-company size
Larger companies provide better payments, reach, exposure, and sponsorship opportunities, but may require:
- Minimum promotion reputation
- Minimum roster and event quality
- Production standards
- Required event frequency
- Geographic restrictions
- Exclusivity

### Deal types
- Television networks
- Streaming platforms
- Regional broadcasters
- Premium or pay-per-view distributors

When scheduling an event, the player chooses the available provider. This affects:
- Revenue
- Audience reach
- Card popularity
- Promotion reputation
- Fighter popularity
- Sponsorship value

### Fighter media ability
Relevant attributes:
- Charisma
- Media training
- Trash talking
- Confidence
- Public speaking
- Humour
- Professionalism
- Marketability

Charismatic fighters can become stars through media exposure. Poor media performers may gain less popularity or damage their public image.

### AI media logic
AI promotions must:
- Find providers
- Negotiate contracts
- Renew deals
- Meet requirements
- Choose providers for events
- Lose exposure if they fail to secure coverage

AI should reassess providers based on:
- Rights payments
- Audience size
- Reputation
- Geographic reach
- Restrictions
- Existing relationships
- Event type
- Profitability

### Dynamic broadcast market
Media companies may:
- Change budgets
- Enter or leave markets
- Expand geographically
- Seek exclusivity
- Target different promotion sizes
- Become more or less selective

## 2. Dynamic Universe Audit

Audit how the game world develops and how systems affect one another.

The goal is a Football Manager-style universe where player and AI choices can create minor or major consequences.

### Core questions
- What affects this system?
- What does it affect?
- Which systems should react?
- Are links missing?
- Are the consequences believable?
- Does the player have real agency?

### Player agency
Meaningful decisions include:
- Signing and releasing fighters
- Matchmaking
- Media deals
- Event locations
- Event quality
- Staff recruitment
- Academy investment
- Regional expansion
- Fighter promotion
- Financial decisions

Consequences may be immediate, delayed, minor, major, positive, negative, or unexpected.

### AI agency
AI organisations should follow the same rules for:
- Rosters
- Finances
- Contracts
- Media
- Events
- Staff
- Talent development
- Championships
- Regional expansion

### Spectator mode
The world should continue developing naturally through:
- Growth
- Decline
- Mistakes
- Recovery
- Dynasties
- Financial collapse
- New stars
- Believable champions

### Audit principle
Begin with an audit, not a rewrite. Preserve systems that already work.

## 3. Rare Mid-Fight Injuries

Add rare, believable injuries tied to actual fight actions.

### Risk factors
- Attempted action
- Existing damage
- Injury history
- Age
- Fatigue
- Weight cut
- Balance and athleticism
- Durability
- Injury proneness
- Cage position
- Opponent contact

### Scenarios
- ACL, MCL, meniscus, patella, Achilles, calf, hamstring, groin, ankle, foot, shin, and toe injuries
- Shoulder, elbow, forearm, hand, wrist, and finger injuries
- Rib, abdominal, back, neck, and spinal injuries
- Orbital, jaw, nose, eye, ear, cut, and concussion injuries
- Submission-related elbow, shoulder, knee, ankle, neck, and rib damage
- Slips, awkward landings, dehydration cramps, and recurring injuries

### Full leg break
A tibia/fibula fracture should be among the rarest events in the game.

Possible outcome:
- Immediate collapse
- Immediate stoppage
- TKO due to injury
- Long recovery
- Possible permanent decline
- Major commentary and media reaction

### Severity
- Minor
- Moderate
- Severe
- Career-threatening

### Official results
- TKO — Injury
- TKO — Doctor Stoppage
- TKO — Corner Stoppage
- Submission
- Technical Submission
- Technical Decision
- No Contest

## 13. Generated Fighter Nationalities and Regional Identity

Audit generated-fighter nationality logic.

Regional youth promotions and academies should mostly produce local fighters, but use weighted probabilities rather than hard restrictions.

### Weighted generation
Consider:
- Promotion location
- Academy location
- Migration patterns
- Neighbouring countries
- International movement
- Family relocation
- Cultural links

### Separate identity fields
- Nationality
- Birth country
- Birth region
- Residence
- Training location
- Fighting base
- Regional popularity
- Cultural or family connections

### Home-event connection levels
- Hometown
- Home region
- National home
- Adopted home
- Training-base connection
- Neutral

These affect:
- Motivation
- Crowd support
- Popularity gain
- Ticket demand
- Event hype
- Media attention

### Regional popularity
Fighters should have popularity values by market, not only globally.

### Location-specific event value
A fight between two major English stars in England may equal or exceed a bout between larger global names because of local demand.

### Hype calculation
Use:
- Regional popularity
- Global popularity
- Rivalry
- Championship stakes
- Rankings
- Win streaks
- Home connection
- Media coverage
- Broadcast reach
- Historical importance

### Regional affinity
Examples:
- Thailand and Southeast Asia
- Thailand and Japanese striking audiences
- Ireland and the UK
- Ireland and parts of North America
- Australia and New Zealand
- Brazil and Portugal
- Mexico and relevant US markets
- The Caucasus, Russia, and Central Asia

Affinity makes popularity growth easier but does not create automatic fame.

### Thai fighters in Japan
Thai fighters can become significant Japanese draws through:
- Muay Thai reputation
- Kickboxing history
- Repeated appearances
- Broadcast exposure
- Strong local performances

The key question is:
> How much does this market care about the fighter?

—not simply:
> Is this fighter from the event’s country?

### AI event planning
AI promotions should:
- Use local and regional stars
- Pair hometown favourites with international names
- Build cards for the local market
- Avoid relying only on global popularity or Overall ratings
